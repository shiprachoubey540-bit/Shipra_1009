About Me

Driven by science, empowered by data

Bridging Biology & Data Science

I'm Shipra, an M.Sc. student at Digital University Kerala, deeply passionate about the intersection of computational biology and machine learning. My work spans molecular docking, network analysis, genomics, and TinyML — all united by one mission: decoding biological complexity with data.

My primary aspiration is to grow as a Bioinformatics Researcher, contributing to drug discovery, genomics, and structural biology. I'm equally eager about Data Scientist roles where my analytical mindset and ML expertise can solve real-world challenges at scale.

Whether it's simulating protein–ligand interactions or crafting predictive models, I bring rigour, curiosity, and relentless ambition to every project.
